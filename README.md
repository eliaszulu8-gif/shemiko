# Shemiko Farm Ledger

Farm records for smallholder farmers and cooperatives in Zambia: crops, poultry, workers, equipment, checklists, money and credit. Works offline on any smartphone browser and installs to the home screen.

Founder: Elias Zulu · eliaszulu74@yahoo.com · +260 969 928 620

## Install on a phone
1. Open the app's web address in Chrome on an Android phone.
2. Tap the menu (⋮), then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
3. Shemiko now opens like a normal app and works without internet. Records are saved on the phone.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole app
- `manifest.webmanifest` – app name, colours and icons for installing
- `sw.js` – offline support (keeps a copy of the app on the phone)
- `icons/` – app icons

## Publish (GitHub Pages)
In the repository: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main / root**. The app appears at `https://<username>.github.io/<repository>/`.
